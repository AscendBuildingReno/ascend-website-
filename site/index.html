Our Story

A Local Renovations Company Built On Experience

From the moment Matthew Anderson picked up a paintbrush as an apprentice, he knew quality craftsmanship mattered. After winning a CITB SkillBuild competition, earning an NVQ Level 3 in Painting & Decorating and completing a foundation degree in construction, he honed his plastering skills working for property developers.

Today, Ascend Building Renovations brings together that hands-on trade background with a practical, organised way of working. From first contact to completion, the focus is on clear advice, tidy workmanship and finishes that stand up over time.

What We Do

Our Decorating & Plastering Services

From painting and decorating to plastering, skimming and wallpaper hanging, we provide the full range of decorating services for homeowners, landlords and commercial clients across Burnley, Southport and the wider North West. Every job is quoted clearly, started on time and finished properly.

Interior Painting & Decorating

Full room redecorations, feature walls, colour changes and fine finishing. Careful preparation, clean sharp results every time.

Exterior Painting & Decorating

Masonry, render, woodwork and metalwork. Protect and refresh the outside of your property with durable exterior finishes.

Wallpaper Hanging & Feature Walls

Expert wallpapering and statement feature walls — patterned, textured and premium papers hung neatly with crisp, seamless joins.

Plastering & Wall Repairs

Skim coating, re-skimming, patch repairs and ceiling finishes. We also repair cracked, damaged and uneven surfaces.

Plasterboard, Ceilings & Structural Prep

Installation and repair of plasterboard, ceiling repairs and surface preparation. Everything underneath done right.

Damp, Mould & Affected Wall Repairs

We fix the damage properly and give clear advice on prevention — not just paint over the problem.

Property Refresh & Maintenance

End-of-tenancy redecorations, void property refreshes and ongoing maintenance for landlords and property managers.
