Skimming Services

Smooth, Paint-Ready Skimming in Burnley

Skimming is the fastest way to turn tired, uneven walls into a flawless surface. One thin finishing coat erases years of cracks, filler and brush marks — and gives paint and paper a perfect base to sit on.

Ascend Building Renovations Ltd provides clean, professional skimming across Burnley, BB11, BB12 and the surrounding East Lancashire towns. Whether you want a single ceiling flattened, an artex finish covered over, or every wall in a room re-skimmed before decorating, the work is carried out by an NVQ Level 3 qualified, CITB SkillBuild award-winning tradesman. You get a glass-smooth finish, a fixed price and a tidy room — every time.

What's Included

  • Wall skimming over sound existing plaster
  • Ceiling skimming for a flat, modern finish
  • Skimming over artex and textured surfaces
  • Re-skimming whole rooms before decorating
  • Over-boarding then skimming where needed
  • Crack and filler made-good before skimming
  • Crisp internal corners, beads and reveals
  • Mist-coat guidance for a first-time paint finish

Our Process

  1. Free Survey & Fixed Quote

    We check the surface, confirm a skim is the right answer, and give you a clear itemised price.

  2. Prepare & Protect

    Floors and furniture sheeted, surfaces cleaned and treated so the new skim bonds properly.

  3. Apply the Skim

    A fine finishing coat trowelled and polished to a flat, smooth, blemish-free surface.

  4. Clean Down & Advise

    Waste removed, room left tidy, with honest advice on drying times before you decorate.

Burnley Skimming FAQ

Skimming applies a thin finishing coat of plaster — usually 2 to 3mm — over an existing wall or ceiling to create a smooth, flat, paint-ready surface. It’s ideal for walls that are sound but tired, patchy, cracked or covered in old paint and filler.

Cost depends on the size of the room, the number of surfaces and their condition. A single wall or ceiling is a small job; a full room is more involved. We give a fixed, itemised quote after a quick look so you know the exact price up front.

Yes — it’s one of the most popular jobs we do. We can skim directly over sound artex or over-board first where needed, leaving a flat, modern ceiling. Pre-2000 textured coatings are handled with appropriate care.

Allow roughly three to seven days for fresh skim to dry fully, depending on weather and ventilation. It turns from dark brown to an even pale pink when ready, and the first coat should be a thinned mist coat.

It depends on the wall. If the existing plaster is solid, a skim is faster, cleaner and cheaper. If it’s blown or hollow, it needs cutting back and re-plastering first. We always advise honestly which your wall needs.

Yes. Skimming flattens fine cracks, old filler, brush marks and lining-paper joints for a seamless surface. Larger structural cracks are cut out and repaired first so they don’t reappear.
