What We Do

Our Services

We cover the full range of decorating and plastering work across Burnley, Southport and the wider North West. Whether it's a single room or a larger renovation, the same standards apply — careful preparation, clean finishes and work you can rely on.

Interior Painting & Decorating

High-quality interior decorating for homes and properties of all sizes. Full room redecorations, feature walls, colour changes and fine finishing, always with careful preparation and clean, sharp results.

Learn More →

Exterior Painting & Decorating

Protect and refresh the outside of your property with durable exterior finishes. We paint masonry, render, woodwork and metalwork, keeping your building looking its best and standing up to the weather.

Learn More →

Plastering & Wall Repairs

Professional plastering including skim coating, re-skimming, patch repairs and ceiling finishes. We also repair cracked, damaged or uneven surfaces to leave walls smooth and ready for decoration.

Learn More →

Plasterboard, Ceilings & Structural Prep

Installation and repair of plasterboard, ceiling repairs and preparation works. Filling, sanding and surface preparation to make sure everything beneath the paint or plaster is done properly.

Learn More →

Damp, Mould & Affected Wall Repairs

Treatment of mould issues and repairs to damp-affected walls. We don't just cover problems up — we fix the damage and give clear advice to help prevent it coming back.

Learn More →

Property Refresh & Maintenance Decorating

Reliable decorating and plastering for landlords, estate agents, care homes and commercial properties. End-of-tenancy redecoration, property refreshes, ongoing maintenance and minor repair works.
