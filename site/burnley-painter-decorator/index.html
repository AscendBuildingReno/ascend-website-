What We Do

Painting, Decorating & Plastering Services in Burnley

Based in Burnley, we cover the full range of painting, decorating and plastering work for homeowners, landlords and commercial clients across BB11, BB12 and the wider East Lancashire area. Every job is quoted clearly, started on time and finished properly.

Interior Painting & Decorating

Full room redecorations, feature walls, colour changes and fine finishing for homes and properties across Burnley. Careful preparation, clean sharp results.

Plastering & Skimming

Skim coating, re-skimming, patch repairs and ceiling finishes throughout BB11 and BB12. Period terraced properties and new builds equally welcome.

Exterior Painting & Decorating

Masonry, render, woodwork and metalwork. Protect and refresh the outside of your Burnley property with durable exterior finishes built to last.

Void Refresh & Landlord Maintenance

End-of-tenancy redecorations and void property refreshes. Fast, reliable turnaround to minimise void periods and keep your rental portfolio compliant.

Damp & Mould Affected Wall Repairs

Repair of damp-affected walls following treatment. We fix the damage properly and give clear advice on prevention — not just a coat of paint over the problem.

Plasterboard, Ceilings & Structural Prep

Installation and repair of plasterboard, ceiling repairs and surface preparation works. Everything underneath done right before a brush touches the wall.
