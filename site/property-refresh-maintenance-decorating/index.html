What We Cover

Property Refresh & Maintenance Services

Void periods cost landlords money. A property that sits empty waiting for a decorator costs more in lost rent than the decorating itself. We work with landlords, letting agents and property managers to turn properties around quickly, to a consistent standard, with clear pricing every time.

Void Property Refresh

Full redecoration of vacant properties between tenancies. Plaster, prep and paint in one package — ready to re-let quickly with a clean, neutral finish.

End-of-Tenancy Redecoration

End-of-tenancy decoration to a move-in standard. Scuffs, marks, holes and damage all dealt with before full redecoration is applied throughout.

Ongoing Maintenance Programme

Scheduled maintenance decorating for landlords with multiple properties. Regular visits to keep your portfolio in condition and avoid costly remedial work later.

Mid-Tenancy Touch-Ups

Minor decoration and repair work carried out around existing tenants with minimal disruption. Keeps properties in good condition throughout a tenancy.

Compliance & HMO Decoration

Decoration for HMOs and licensed properties maintained to the standards required by selective licensing and the Renters Rights Act.

Letting Agent Preferred Supplier

We work with letting agents on agreed rates and preferred supplier arrangements. Consistent quality, reliable scheduling and prompt invoicing.

For Landlords & Letting Agents

Your Trusted Maintenance Decorating Partner

With the Renters Rights Act now in force and selective licensing schemes operating across parts of Lancashire and Merseyside, keeping your properties maintained isn't optional — it's a legal requirement. We help landlords and agents stay compliant, reduce void periods and keep tenants happy.

  • Void refreshes completed to a fast, agreed turnaround
  • End-of-tenancy redecorations — plaster, prep and paint in one
  • Renters Rights Act compliance — properties maintained to required standards
  • Damp, mould and damage repairs handled in the same visit
  • Flexible scheduling around existing tenants
  • Clear fixed pricing for agents and portfolio landlords
