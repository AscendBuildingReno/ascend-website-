Our Story

Built On Experience, Driven By Craft

Most people looking for a decorator or plasterer want the same things: work done properly, clear communication and no unnecessary stress.

Ascend Building Renovations was founded by Matthew Anderson, who began his career through a traditional painting and decorating apprenticeship straight after leaving school. During his training, Matthew went on to win a CITB SkillBuild competition, recognising both technical skill and attention to detail.

After qualifying, he worked for a housing association and later for a property developer and landlord, where he expanded his skill set to include plastering, damp-related repairs and broader renovation work. Alongside this, he achieved an NVQ Level 3 in Painting & Decorating and a Foundation Degree in Construction.

Today, Ascend Building Renovations brings together that hands-on trade background with a practical, organised way of working. From first contact to completion, the focus is on clear advice, tidy workmanship and finishes that stand up over time.
