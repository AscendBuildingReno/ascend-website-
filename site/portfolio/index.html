Main Bedroom & Artex Ceiling Skimming in Padiham, Burnley

For this main bedroom skimming job in Padiham, Burnley, Ascend Building Renovations skimmed the walls and plastered over the existing Artex ceiling to create a smooth, consistent finish. The seven project photos show the original painted walls and textured ceiling alongside the freshly skimmed surfaces, including the doorway, corners and ceiling around the light fittings.

The preparation used a mix of PVA and SBR before multi-surface plaster was applied to the bedroom walls and ceiling. Preparing the existing surfaces helps the new skim bond and manages suction, giving the plaster a suitable base. Skimming over the Artex changed the textured ceiling to a smooth finish and brought the room together with the newly plastered walls.

The finished photographs show fresh plaster before decoration. Once the skim has dried fully, the bedroom can be prepared for painting with an appropriate mist coat and the chosen decorative finish. For homeowners looking for a plasterer in Padiham or bedroom skimming near Burnley, this project shows the difference a full wall and ceiling skim can make.

Can you paint over nicotine and tobacco stained walls in Burnley and Southport?

Yes, but not with emulsion alone. Nicotine dissolves into water-based paint and bleeds back through as brown patches. We seal every stained wall and ceiling with a stain block primer first, then finish in a hard-wearing paint such as vinyl matt magnolia, as shown in the nicotine-stained property refresh in this gallery. We carry out stain blocking and redecoration for homeowners, landlords and letting agents across Burnley, East Lancashire, Southport, Birkdale, Ainsdale, Formby and Ormskirk.
