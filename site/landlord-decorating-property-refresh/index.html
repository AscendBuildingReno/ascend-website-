Decorating & Refresh Services Built Around Rentals

Every empty week costs you money, and tired properties let slowly. Ascend Building Renovations Ltd gives landlords and letting agents a single, reliable trade partner who turns properties around fast, keeps them compliant, and finishes them to a standard that re-lets quickly.

We're based in Burnley and work right across East Lancashire and the Southport coast, so landlords with properties in either area — or both — can use one team for everything. From a quick end-of-tenancy refresh to a full void renovation, plaster repairs, damp work and ongoing maintenance, you get clear itemised pricing, dependable turnarounds and decoration that's built to last between tenants. With the Renters Rights Act now in force and selective licensing operating in parts of Burnley, keeping your properties maintained to standard has never mattered more — and that's exactly what we help you do.

What We Do for Landlords

  • End-of-tenancy and void redecoration
  • Fast turnarounds to minimise void periods
  • Plaster repairs, skimming and making good
  • Damp and mould repair — fixed, not painted over
  • Hard-wearing, neutral decoration that re-lets well
  • Pre-let presentation and full property refreshes
  • Ongoing maintenance across your portfolio
  • Direct working with letting and managing agents

Our Process

  1. Free Survey & Fixed Quote

    We assess the property (or your whole portfolio), agree priorities and give clear itemised pricing with realistic turnaround times.

  2. Rapid Mobilisation

    We schedule void work fast — the sooner it's done, the sooner the property is back on the market and earning.

  3. Repair, Prep & Decorate

    Plaster, damp and woodwork sorted first, then durable, neutral decoration applied to a consistent standard.

  4. Ready to Re-Let

    Snagged, cleaned and handed back viewing-ready, so your agent can market it straight away.
