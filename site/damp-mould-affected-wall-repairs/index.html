What We Cover

Damp & Mould Repair Services

Damp and mould damage is one of the most common — and most badly fixed — problems in UK housing. Painting over affected plaster doesn't solve it. We repair the damage properly once the source has been treated, using the right materials and the right approach for long-lasting results.

Damp-Affected Wall Repair

Removal of damaged plaster, treatment of the substrate and re-plastering to a sound finish. Carried out after the source of damp has been addressed.

Mould Removal & Surface Treatment

Thorough mould removal, surface sanitisation and application of mould-inhibiting treatments before any new plaster or decoration is applied.

Salt-Stained Plaster Repair

Removal and replacement of salt-contaminated plaster — salt deposits from rising damp keep attacking new decoration if the underlying plaster isn't replaced.

Moisture Barrier Application

Application of specialist moisture-blocking primers and treatments to walls where residual moisture is present following damp-proofing works.

Post-Leak Repair

Repair of walls and ceilings following plumbing leaks, roof leaks or water ingress. Stabilised, re-plastered and decorated to match.

Landlord Compliance Repairs

Damp and mould repairs for rental properties in line with Renters Rights Act obligations. Fast turnaround, clear pricing, documented work completion.

Our Approach

Fix It Properly, Not Temporarily

The number one mistake with damp-affected walls is decorating over them without removing the damaged material underneath. Salt contamination, compromised bond and residual moisture will keep reappearing through any new surface. We do it right — even when it takes longer.

  • Honest assessment of what caused the damage and whether it's been fixed
  • Affected plaster hacked back to sound substrate
  • Substrate treated and dried before any new material applied
  • Correct backing coats and finish plaster for damp-prone surfaces
  • Clear advice on ventilation and prevention going forward
  • Landlords: documented completion for compliance records
