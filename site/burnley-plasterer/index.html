Plastering Services

Professional Plastering & Skimming in Burnley

A good decorating job is only ever as good as the surface underneath it. Ascend Building Renovations Ltd provides clean, level, properly finished plastering across Burnley — the foundation that lets paint and paper sit flawlessly for years.

We're based on Wellfield Drive in Burnley and work throughout BB11, BB12 and the surrounding East Lancashire towns. Whether it's a single blown patch in a hallway, a tired ceiling that needs flattening, or a full re-skim of a through-lounge in a Victorian terrace, the work is carried out by a time-served, NVQ Level 3 qualified tradesman — not passed to a subcontractor. You get a smooth, flat, ready-to-decorate finish and a tidy room at the end of every day.

What's Included

  • Skim coating over sound existing plaster
  • Full re-plastering of blown or failed walls
  • Ceiling skimming and over-boarding of artex
  • Patch repairs, cracks and made-good after building work
  • Plasterboard supply, fixing and taping (dry lining)
  • Reveals, beads and crisp internal corners
  • Made-good around new sockets, doorways and openings
  • Mist-coat advice so your paint bonds first time

Our Process

  1. Free Survey & Fixed Quote

    We look at the room, check what the walls actually need, and give you a clear itemised price — no guesswork, no surprises.

  2. Protect & Prepare

    Floors and furniture are sheeted, loose and blown plaster is cut back, and surfaces are treated so the new plaster bonds properly.

  3. Plaster & Skim

    Base coats where needed, then a fine finishing skim worked to a flat, polished surface ready for decoration.

  4. Clean Down & Advise

    Waste removed, room left tidy, and you get straightforward advice on drying times before painting.

Burnley Plastering FAQ

Cost depends on the size of the room, the condition of the existing walls and whether it’s a skim over sound plaster or a full re-plaster. Skimming a single bedroom ceiling is a smaller job than re-skimming a whole through-lounge. Every quote is fixed and itemised after we’ve seen the room, so there are no surprises.

Skimming applies a thin 2–3mm finishing coat over walls that are already sound. Re-plastering means removing failed plaster back to the brick and building it back up with a base coat before skimming. We’ll tell you honestly which your walls actually need.

Fresh plaster usually needs three to seven days to dry fully, longer in cold or damp conditions. It turns from dark brown to an even pale pink when ready. We recommend a thinned mist coat first so the paint bonds rather than peels.

Yes. We regularly skim or over-board artex ceilings for a flat, modern finish. Sound artex can be skimmed directly; loose areas are prepared first, and any pre-2000 coating is treated with appropriate care.
