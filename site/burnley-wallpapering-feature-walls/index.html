Wallpapering Services

Expert Wallpaper Hanging & Feature Walls in Burnley

Wallpaper transforms a room like nothing else — but only when it's hung properly. Lifted seams, mismatched patterns and bubbles are the difference between a job that looks expensive and one that looks rushed. We take the time to get it right.

Ascend Building Renovations Ltd hangs wallpaper and creates statement feature walls for homeowners and landlords across Burnley and East Lancashire. From a single bold accent wall in a lounge to fully papered hallways, bedrooms and made-to-measure murals, every job is carried out by an NVQ Level 3 qualified decorator with the patience and precision that good paper-hanging demands. The result is a flawless, premium finish that lifts the whole room.

What's Included

  • Statement feature walls and accent walls
  • Full-room and hallway wallpapering
  • Premium, heavyweight and textured papers
  • Paste-the-wall and traditional papers
  • Made-to-measure murals and panel sets
  • Precise pattern matching and drop repeats
  • Old wallpaper stripping and wall make-good
  • Lining, sizing and full surface preparation

Our Process

  1. Free Consultation & Quote

    We look at the wall, talk through paper options and quantities, and give you a fixed price before anything is ordered.

  2. Prepare the Surface

    Fill, sand, size and line as needed so the paper sits dead flat with no imperfections showing through.

  3. Plan & Hang

    Layout set out so patterns and murals land centred and balanced, then hung with tight, invisible seams.

  4. Finish & Clean Down

    Edges trimmed crisp, surfaces wiped down, room left clean and ready to enjoy.
