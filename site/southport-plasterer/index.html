Plastering Services

Professional Plastering & Skimming in Southport

A flawless paint finish always starts with a flawless wall. Ascend Building Renovations Ltd provides clean, level, properly finished plastering across Southport — the solid foundation that lets your decoration look its best for years.

We work regularly throughout Southport and the West Lancashire coast, covering PR8, PR9, Birkdale, Ainsdale, Churchtown and Formby. From a single blown patch to a tired ceiling or a full re-skim of a period reception room, the work is carried out by a time-served, NVQ Level 3 qualified tradesman — not handed to a subcontractor. Southport's mix of Victorian villas, coastal semis and modern homes each have their quirks, and 15+ years of experience means we handle them all. You get a smooth, ready-to-decorate finish and a tidy room every day.

What's Included

  • Skim coating over sound existing plaster
  • Full re-plastering of blown or failed walls
  • Ceiling skimming and over-boarding of artex
  • Patch repairs, cracks and made-good after works
  • Damp-affected plaster repair after treatment
  • Plasterboard supply, fixing and taping (dry lining)
  • Reveals, beads and crisp internal corners
  • Mist-coat advice so your paint bonds first time

Our Process

  1. Free Survey & Fixed Quote

    We look at the room, check what the walls actually need, and give a clear itemised price — no guesswork.

  2. Protect & Prepare

    Floors and furniture sheeted, loose plaster cut back, surfaces treated so new plaster bonds properly.

  3. Plaster & Skim

    Base coats where needed, then a fine finishing skim worked flat and ready for decoration.

  4. Clean Down & Advise

    Waste removed, room left tidy, with honest advice on drying times before painting.
