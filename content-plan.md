# Content plan: Ascend blog

Two posts a week (from 1 Dec 2026):
- **Tuesday:** Southport-focused (Southport, Birkdale, Ainsdale, Churchtown, Formby, PR8/PR9).
- **Thursday:** Burnley-focused (Burnley, Padiham, Nelson, Colne, East Lancashire), plus portfolio projects from Matty's WhatsApp photos.

## Rules
- Each post answers one real question a homeowner or landlord would search, in roughly 700–1,200 words, with practical and accurate steps.
- Never invent jobs, prices, reviews or facts. Link to real portfolio projects only where they're relevant.
- No near-duplicates. Never swap the town name on an existing post.
- Link to the matching service page, plus a call to action to /contact/ and 07417 424115.
